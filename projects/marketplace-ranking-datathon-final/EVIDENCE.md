# Evidence and publication scope

The case study was prepared by privately reviewing a team presentation and source archive. Neither is distributed or linked from this portfolio.

## Claim and evidence boundaries

| Portfolio claim | Basis | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Real-estate recommendation task and team approach | Team presentation and source review | Describes the team's design, not independently reproduced performance |
| Candidate retrieval, LambdaRank, marketplace score adjustment, and format checks | Inspection of privately supplied implementation | Internal schema, configuration, code paths, and numerical outcomes are omitted |
| Internal validation differs from a full later-period evaluation | Review of training and evaluation logic | No independent pipeline backtest or leakage audit was completed |
| Assigned technical contribution and presentation/defense | Participant-confirmed scope | Does not establish sole ownership of pipeline or marketplace data analysis |
| Third Prize, DATATHON 2026 Final | Participant-reported team achievement | The supplied presentation is not an award certificate |

## Review and attribution

Source documentation, training and ranking logic, validation logic, and presentation content were reviewed. The team pipeline and archived tests were not run, and original data was not loaded. Tests in the separate synthetic recommender project do not validate this competition pipeline.

The original implementation, slides, and competition materials remain team work. This portfolio publishes a personal case study and explanatory review only. It does not redistribute datasets, identifiers, feature tables, trained models, submissions, figures, exact user behavior rules, benchmark outputs, or original source files.
