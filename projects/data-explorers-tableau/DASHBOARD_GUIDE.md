# Dashboard walkthrough

[Case study](README.md) · [Metric definitions](METRICS.md) · [Evidence](EVIDENCE.md)

This guide proposes a way to explain the three team dashboard pages during an authorized local review. It publishes no original workbook view or data value.

## Business results

Start with premium totals and entity counts. Then check trends and mix by period, product, and channel. Ask whether a change reflects volume, average premium, or selection of the reporting period. Premium revenue alone does not measure profit.

## Customers and products

Review segment distribution as a starting point for investigation. Any customer grouping produced by a simple rule is descriptive; a label implying risk or purchase propensity needs separate validation.

## KPI and sales effectiveness

Compare actual and target over the same time and organizational scope. Check the sign of the target gap and the meaning of each threshold before making a management recommendation. Employee groupings based on observed sales are not a causal assessment of performance.

## Checks before presenting

Open the original workbook only in an environment authorized for the competition data. Confirm metric labels, period selection, filters, and layout in Tableau. The portfolio review inspected workbook structure but did not test the interactive dashboard or recompute financial totals. No public workbook or live Tableau link is provided.
