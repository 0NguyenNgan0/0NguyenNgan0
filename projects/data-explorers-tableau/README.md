# Insurance Business Intelligence with Tableau
### Data Explorers 2026 · Team Serendipity · Data storytelling

A team dashboard project connecting insurance business results, customer and product analysis, and performance targets to management questions.

**My contribution:** data cleaning and modeling, coordination of task allocation, and structuring the presentation content.

## Business context and team artifact

The team organized business questions into three Tableau dashboard pages covering overall results, customer and product segments, and target attainment. The privately reviewed workbook has **3 dashboards and 26 worksheets**. These counts describe the team artifact, not what I implemented alone.

| View | Management question |
| --- | --- |
| Business results | How do totals, mix, and time trends compare? |
| Customers and products | Which segments may need further investigation? |
| KPI and sales effectiveness | Where are observed outcomes below the stated target? |

These are questions supported by the workbook design. No revenue uplift, improved retention, validated risk score, or deployed business outcome is claimed.

## My role

I contributed to cleaning and modeling the data, helped coordinate team tasks, and structured the presentation. The workbook and its calculated fields are shared team work; I do not claim to have built every dashboard or calculation.

A useful lesson is to distinguish the share of existing contracts marked as renewals from a cohort retention rate. The latter needs an eligible group and a follow-up period. See the [metric notes](METRICS.md) and [review findings](EVIDENCE.md).

## Public portfolio scope

The competition materials are kept private. This case study shares the analytical questions, high-level metric definitions, and review observations without the original workbook, data, internal schema, formulas, source names, extracts, screenshots, or business values.

The workbook was inspected structurally; it has not been opened and tested interactively in Tableau during this review. This page is a documented case study, not a live dashboard demo.
